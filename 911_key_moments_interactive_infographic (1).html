Historical timeline • September 11, 2001

Key moments of the World Trade Center attack

A concise, non-graphic timeline of the morning's major events, with the World Trade Center at its center and the other two attack sites included for context.

Content note: This page describes a mass-casualty terrorist attack. It avoids graphic imagery and details.
8:46 a.m. EDT
Flight 11 strikes the North Tower
American Airlines Flight 11 was deliberately flown into floors 93–99 of 1 World Trade Center.
The impact began the sequence of events at the World Trade Center that morning. Emergency personnel and civilians immediately began responding and evacuating.
9:03 a.m.
Flight 175 strikes the South Tower
United Airlines Flight 175 was deliberately flown into floors 77–85 of 2 World Trade Center.
The second impact made clear that the country was facing a coordinated attack. Evacuations and rescue efforts at the complex continued.
9:37 a.m.
Flight 77 strikes the Pentagon
American Airlines Flight 77 was deliberately crashed into the Pentagon in Arlington, Virginia.
This was the third of the four hijacked-aircraft attacks that morning.
9:59 a.m.
South Tower collapses
The South Tower collapsed 56 minutes after it was struck.
The collapse devastated the surrounding World Trade Center site and created an enormous emergency response.
10:03 a.m.
Flight 93 crashes in Pennsylvania
Passengers and crew attempted to regain control of United Airlines Flight 93; the plane crashed near Shanksville, Pennsylvania.
The aircraft had been headed toward Washington, D.C. The passenger counterattack prevented it from reaching its intended target, according to the 9/11 Commission.
10:28 a.m.
North Tower collapses
The North Tower collapsed, bringing the morning's destruction of the Twin Towers to an end.
Rescue, recovery, and relief operations continued immediately and would continue at the World Trade Center site for months.
