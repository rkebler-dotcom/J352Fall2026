Woodstock: Peace to Chaos

A famous exhibit of '60s counterculture and celebration of peace, the 1969 Woodstock Music & Art Fair in Bethel, New York presented four days of performances from Jimi Hendrix, Janis Joplin and more. In an attempt to bring back that feeling music-lovers felt many years ago, another Woodstock festival was held in 1999. Near the end of the festival, attendees grew sick of the poor management of this years' festival and began rioting. A series of fires and violence broke out that night, making the festival a major conversation in music to this day.

Woodstock over the years
